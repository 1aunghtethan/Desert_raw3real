# Desert Raw 3 - 7-Day Social Campaign

## Campaign Goal

Build followers and community before a Steam page exists.

Positioning: Desert Raw 3 is a desert survival sandbox where the desert itself is the enemy: moving sand, heat, shade, roots/shelter, oasis relief, hunting, and crafting.

Primary CTA:

- Follow for the next desert survival test.
- Comment what I should add or fix.

Primary metrics:

- New followers
- Comments
- Shares/saves
- Reddit replies
- Community joins, if a Discord or group link is available

## Platform Rules

TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and Instagram Reels:

- Use 8-25 second vertical clips.
- Show gameplay in the first 1-2 seconds.
- Use a strong on-screen hook.
- End with a loop or open question.
- Avoid developer-only explanations unless the visual is impressive.

X:

- Attach gameplay media whenever possible.
- Use one clear question.
- Reply to every useful comment within the first hour.

Reddit:

- Post as a developer asking for feedback, not as an advertiser.
- Use transparent wording.
- Avoid repeated links unless the community rules allow them.
- Prefer genre and development communities that permit feedback posts.

## Daily Calendar

### Day 1 - Hook Reveal

Goal: Make the core fantasy clear.

Clip:

- Player alone in desert.
- Heat or thirst pressure visible.
- Sand moving or changing.

Short-form script:

> Most survival games use desert as a background. Mine makes it the enemy.

On-screen text:

> The desert is not a map. It is the enemy.

TikTok caption:

> I am building a desert survival sandbox where the terrain can fight back. Follow for the next survival test. #indiegame #survivalgame #gamedev #sandboxgame

YouTube Shorts title:

> The Desert Is the Enemy in My Survival Game

Instagram Reels caption:

> I want the desert to feel alive, dangerous, and changeable. What should it do to the player?

X post:

> Most survival games use desert as a background. I want mine to make the desert the enemy: heat, sand, shade, and survival choices. What is the first thing a living desert should do to the player?

Reddit draft:

> I am making a desert survival sandbox where sand can change the world instead of just being scenery. I want the desert itself to feel like the main threat: heat, shade, terrain, water, and shelter all matter.

Checklist:

- Open with gameplay, not logo or menu.
- Confirm the player/desert threat is understandable with sound off.
- Pin or save the best comment for Day 7.

## Weekly Review

After Day 3:

- Pick the clearest mechanic from Days 1-3.
- Repeat that mechanic with a new angle.
- Fix unclear text, UI, or edit pacing before Days 4-7.

After Day 7:

- Identify the top hook by comments/follows, not views alone.
- Turn the winner into next week's main content pillar.
- Save player wording for future store-page copy.
