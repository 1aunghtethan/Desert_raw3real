# Desert Raw 3 Daily Social Checklist Automation Prompt

Prepare today's Desert Raw 3 social campaign checklist.

Campaign rules:

- Use the 7-day campaign day number, starting with Day 1 on the first scheduled run and advancing one day per run.
- Keep the campaign focused on followers and community, not Steam wishlists.
- Do not auto-post.
- Produce copy drafts only.

For the current day, generate:

1. A 5-item capture checklist for the gameplay clip.
2. One 8-25 second short-form script.
3. TikTok caption and hashtags.
4. YouTube Shorts title, description, and pinned comment.
5. Instagram Reels caption.
6. X post.
7. Reddit post draft.
8. A measurement checklist covering views, retention, comments, shares/saves, follows, and best comment.
9. One end-of-day decision: keep, repeat, revise, or drop the hook.

Campaign days:

- Day 1: Hook Reveal
- Day 2: Sand Physics
- Day 3: Root/Shelter Mechanic
- Day 4: Survival Pressure
- Day 5: Oasis Relief
- Day 6: Hunt/Craft Loop
- Day 7: Community Choice
